Privacy Policy

App: Subscription Tracker - Manage & Remind

Developer: yoshida-web-office

Last updated: May 28, 2026

This policy explains how the app handles your information. 日本語版はこちら。

1. Information We Collect and How We Use It

1-1. Information you enter (stored on your device only)

The app stores the following information only in a local database (Room) on your device. This subscription data is never sent to any external server.

  • Service name
  • Amount
  • Billing cycle (monthly / yearly / weekly)
  • Next billing date
  • Category
  • Reminder settings
  • Free-trial end date
  • Notes

This information is used only for the app's features (spending overview, billing-date reminder notifications, and statistics) and for no other purpose.

1-2. Information collected automatically

To improve quality and serve ads, the app may collect the following through third-party services. These do not include your specific subscription content such as service names, amounts, or notes.

  • Usage analytics (Firebase Analytics): anonymous statistics such as screen views and feature usage counts
  • Crash information (Firebase Crashlytics): technical data when the app crashes
  • Advertising identifier (Ad ID) (Google AdMob): for ad delivery

2. Third-Party Services

The app uses the following third-party services. Please review each service's own privacy policy for how they handle data.

  • Google AdMob (ad delivery)
    https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Firebase Analytics (usage analytics)
    https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Firebase Crashlytics (crash reporting)
    https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

3. Data Sharing

The subscription information you enter (service names, amounts, notes, etc.) is stored only on your device and is never shared with or sent to any third party.

Firebase Analytics data is handled only as anonymous, aggregated statistics that cannot identify an individual.

4. Advertising

The app displays ads via Google AdMob. AdMob may use an advertising identifier (Ad ID) to personalize ads. You can opt out of ad personalization in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

5. Deleting Your Data

  • Delete individually: remove each subscription within the app
  • Delete all: Device Settings → Apps → Subscription Tracker → Storage → Clear data
  • Uninstall: uninstalling the app removes all data from your device

6. Disclaimer

This app is a tool to help you manage subscriptions. The amounts, billing dates, and statistics shown are reference values based on the information you enter. Please check each service's official information for actual billing. The developer is not liable for any damages arising from use of this app.

This policy may be revised as needed. We will notify you of significant changes within the app or on the store listing.
